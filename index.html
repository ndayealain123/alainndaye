🧪 Testing Methodology

All assessments follow strict ethical guidelines and industry standards:

1. Reconnaissance & Mapping: Discovering endpoints, parameters, and hidden directories using FFUF and Gobuster.
2. Traffic Analysis: Intercepting and modifying HTTP requests/responses via Burp Suite.
3. Manual Verification: Confirming findings manually to eliminate false positives.
4. Impact & Reporting: Documenting CVSS scores, step-by-step reproduction instructions, and actionable remediation guidance.
Disclaimer: All testing documented in this portfolio was performed within authorized, isolated lab environments (such as OWASP Juice Shop, DVWA, and custom local containers) with explicit permission.
